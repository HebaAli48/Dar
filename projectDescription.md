# DAR | دار — Project Description

## About DAR

**DAR** (دار) is a smart student housing platform designed to help university students find **affordable, safe, and compatible housing** near their universities. The platform connects students, landlords, and roommates through a verified digital marketplace and provides tools that simplify the entire process of renting shared housing.

> **"DAR connects students to safe, affordable housing with compatible roommates — saving time, money, and stress."**

---

## The Problem

University students face significant difficulties when searching for housing:

- **Unverified listings** on social media leading to scams and financial loss
- **High rental prices** causing budget stress
- **Random or incompatible roommates** resulting in living conflicts
- **Hidden costs** creating unexpected financial burdens
- **No central trusted platform**, wasting students' time
- **Lack of safety information** leading to unsafe living conditions

---

## The Solution

DAR solves these problems through four key pillars:

### 1. Financial Transparency
- Budget-friendly housing options with clear and transparent pricing
- Smart budget filters that suggest apartments based on student budgets
- Flexible and multiple payment methods (monthly, shared, digital)

### 2. Social Compatibility
- Personality-based roommate matching algorithm (sleep schedule, cleanliness, study habits, social preferences)
- "Complete the Flat" feature to find additional roommates for shared apartments

### 3. Safety & Trust
- Verified listings with ID, ownership, and photo verification for landlords
- Safety ratings for neighborhoods (lighting, security, nearby services)
- Real reviews and ratings from previous tenants

### 4. Convenience & Technology
- Smart housing search by location, price, roommates, and distance to campus
- Interactive smart map showing distance, travel time, transport, and nearby services
- 360° immersive room views before visiting
- In-app messaging with landlords and roommates

---

## Unique Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Smart Map** | Interactive map with distance, travel time, transport options, and nearby services |
| **Roommate Matcher** | Personality questionnaire + compatibility algorithm |
| **360° Room View** | Immersive 360-degree views of rooms and apartments |
| **Legacy Housing** | Students leaving apartments can transfer leases, furniture, or full setups to new students |
| **Student Marketplace** | Buy and sell used furniture and appliances locally |
| **Verified Listings** | ID, ownership, and photo verification for landlords |
| **Safety Rating** | Neighborhood safety indicators |

## Target Users

### Primary
- University students (18–25 years old)
- Students moving to new cities
- Young professionals seeking shared housing

### Secondary
- Parents searching for safe housing for their children
- Landlords renting apartments to students
- Universities providing housing support
